Smart Dorm Energy Saver

An AI agent for an electric heater in an Astana dorm room in winter. It plans when to heat, checks whether the student is home, and switches the heater off when the room is probably empty.

1 Hill climbing → daily plan→ 2 Bayes → P(student is home)→ 3 MDP → Keep On / Turn Off→ 4 One day → tenge saved

1Plan the day hill climbing

Cost of a schedule = electricity (hours × 37.5 tg) + discomfort penalty. Move to the cheaper neighbour until none is cheaper.

    2Is the student home? Bayes

    The heater is on by plan, and the motion sensor reports. Bayes' rule updates the belief.

    3Keep on or turn off? MDP

    Rewards per hour for each state and action. The belief from step 2 weights them: Q(a) = Σ b(s)·R(s,a).

    4One winter day plan + Bayes + MDP every hour

    Every hour, the sensor reading goes through Bayes and the MDP. Inside the plan (underlined hours) the prior is 0.6, so the agent switches the heater off when the room is probably empty. Outside the plan the prior is 0.3, but if it sees the student it switches the heater on. Click an hour to flip its sensor reading.
